## Bass, acoustic guitar and strings

- Distribution and curation: [Nathan Brosowsky / tonejs-instruments](https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments).
- The collection declares its samples [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
- Source attribution from the upstream collection: bass from [Karoryfer](https://www.karoryfer.com/karoryfer-samples); acoustic guitar from the [University of Iowa Musical Instrument Samples](http://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/); violin from [Versilian Studios VSCO Community](http://vis.versilstudios.net/vsco-community.html).
- Upstream documentation is preserved in `public/licenses/INSTRUMENT-SAMPLES-README.md` and `public/licenses/INSTRUMENT-SOURCES.txt`.
- TapComposer includes nine bass, twelve acoustic guitar and nine violin samples. Bass and guitar are trimmed to at most five seconds, violin to eight seconds, and re-encoded as mono 44.1 kHz 96 kbps MP3. Playback transposes, envelopes and mixes these samples; strings sustain using a loop and the mixer adds room ambience. These are modifications by TapComposer, not an endorsement by the original creators.

## MP3 encoding

`@breezystack/lamejs` is an LGPL-3.0 licensed fork of Alex Zhukov's JavaScript LAME port. It is bundled as a separate worker and is not modified.

- [Fork and corresponding source](https://github.com/shijinyu/lamejs)
- [Upstream](https://github.com/zhuker/lamejs)
- License copy: `public/licenses/LAMEJS-LICENSE.txt`.
- The package source and build instructions are available from the fork. Replace the dependency and rebuild the app to use a modified compatible encoder.

## Generated sounds

The deterministic kick, snare, hi-hat, synth tone and room impulse are generated by TapComposer's own DSP. No external drum recordings are used.

Other JavaScript dependencies retain their license notices in their source packages and any emitted bundler license notices.
